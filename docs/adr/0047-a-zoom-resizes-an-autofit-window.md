# A Zoom Resizes an AutoFit Window

**Zoom Resizes Window** is a **Window Setting** (ADR-0038), off by default, that only does something in a Normal window with **AutoFit** on: the window then takes the size of the zoomed image, the fitted size times the **Zoom Factor**, no larger than the **Available Area** and no smaller than the window's minimum size (issue #283). The rule is part of the **Fit**. `FitInput.WindowZoomFactor` carries the factor the window is sized at, or 0 when the window does not follow the zoom, and `ImageFitter` gives the window size from it, with two more values on the `Fit`: the area the zoomed image is shown in (`ViewportWidth`, `ViewportHeight`, before the image's quarter turns, as `Width` and `Height` are) and the factor from which the window has stopped growing because the zoomed image fills the Available Area both ways (`WindowGrowthLimit`). The image keeps its fitted size and its **Fit Scale**, so the **Zoom Level** is still the Fit Scale times the Zoom Factor (ADR-0016).

The window follows the zoom as it follows every other input of its Fit (ADR-0026): `WindowFitter.Follow()` subscribes to the setting, `WindowSettings.IsZoomResizingWindow`, and to the active tab's Zoom Factor, `TabZoom.FactorValue`, and nothing asks for a Fit from where the zoom changed. A factor change fits only while the setting is on and the window is AutoFit, so a zoom step costs nothing anywhere else. Every writer of the Zoom Factor counts, the wheel, a pinch, the zoom Actions, the reset and the tab's own reset as it shows another image; with zoom kept across images, the next image opens at its fitted size times the kept factor. The fitter passes the factor only while the tab's **Tab View** is the image viewer, since the crop (ADR-0027) and the comparison (ADR-0032) zoom through controls of their own, and `ImageFitter` ignores it for a window that keeps its size, a fullscreen one, and while scrolling is on.

The viewer shows the result through its zoom control. `MainWindowViewModel.ViewportWidth` and `ViewportHeight` are NaN unless the Fit gave a viewport, and `ImageViewer.axaml` binds the `ZoomPanControl`'s `Width` and `Height` to them. A `ZoomPanControl` that has a size of its own measures its child unconstrained and arranges it at its own size at the control's origin, and constrains the translation again on every arrange, against the size it is being given. So the image is centered in the viewport while it fits, which is the whole time the window is still growing or shrinking, and it keeps its pan inside the viewport once the window has stopped at the Available Area. The window is still sized by its content (`WindowSizingPlan` is unchanged) and placed by the after-resize rule of ADR-0026, on its own center or the **Work Area**'s with **Stay Centered**.

A zoom step that resizes the window is applied at once: `ZoomPanControl.ResizesWindow` says so when either end of the step is below `WindowGrowthLimit`, and the control then sets no transitions, whatever "animate zoom" says. The animation plays only for zooming inside a window that has stopped growing.

## Considered Options

- **Independent of AutoFit**, so a zoom resizes a window that keeps its size. That window is the one whose size the user gave it; a zoom would take it away, and navigation would leave the window at whatever size the last zoom gave. Rejected: the setting is a sub-option of AutoFit, as Stay Centered is, and its toggle is disabled while AutoFit is off.
- **Resizing the window zooms the image**, the other reading of the issue's title. A window that keeps its size with Zoom to Fit on already rescales its image as its border is dragged.
- **Only the wheel, or only with a modifier held**, resizes the window. It would need a second zoom path that knows where a zoom came from; the Zoom Factor is one value with several writers, and the fitter follows the value.
- **The pixel under the cursor stays put on screen**, so the window grows and moves around the cursor. It fights Stay Centered and can push the window off its screen. The whole image is visible while the window grows, so the anchor adds nothing there; zooming goes toward the cursor again once the window has stopped.
- **The native window resizes on every frame of an animated zoom.** Each resize also places the window, and per-frame native resizes stutter on Windows and macOS.
- **Lay the image out at the zoomed size** and keep the control's scale at 1 while the window grows. The Zoom Factor would be split between layout and the control's transform, and every reader of the control's scale, the viewer's report to the tab, the zoom previewer, the pan rule, would have to add the two.
- **Never below the fitted size.** With "avoid zooming out" on, the control already keeps the factor at 1 or more, so the window never goes below its fitted size. With it off, the same rule runs both ways and the window shrinks with the image to its minimum size.

## Consequences

- Updates ADR-0026: a zoom changes an AutoFit window's size while this setting is on, and the fitter's inputs gain the setting and the active tab's Zoom Factor. ADR-0016 holds; `TabZoom.Factor` now reads a reactive property, `FactorValue`, which publishes on the thread that wrote it.
- With the setting on and scrolling off, an AutoFit window's viewer always has a viewport, also at a factor of 1, where it equals the fitted size. With it off, or in any other mode, the viewport is NaN and the control lays out as before.
- A new image while zoom resets gives two Fits when the factor was not 1: the new image's, and one for the factor's reset. They give the same size.
- Zooming while scrolling is on is left as it was, and the window does not follow it.
- A tab that shows no image keeps the size the last Fit gave the window, which may be a zoomed size.
- Resizing the window by hand still turns AutoFit off; the setting is remembered and does nothing until AutoFit is on again.
- The step that crosses the growth limit is not anchored exactly to the cursor: its translation was worked out against the size the control had before the Fit, and is constrained to the new one.
- The setting is `WindowProperties.ZoomResizesWindow` in the record, read, copied and stored with the other Window Settings; the settings file keeps its version. Its toggle in the Settings window's Window page binds two-way to the Active Main Window's value, as Stay Centered's does, since no Action writes it (ADR-0020), and `SettingBindingContractTests` exempts it by name.
- Tested through `ImageFitterTests` (the sizes), `WindowFitterTests` (each case through the headless window, with content that takes the viewport's size), `ZoomPanControlViewportTests` (the control's layout and the animation rule), `WindowSettingsTests` and `WindowSettingsViewTests`. Not run in the real app on any platform when this was written.
