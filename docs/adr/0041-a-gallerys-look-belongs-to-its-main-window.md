# A Gallery's Look Belongs to Its Main Window

Eight more **Settings**, all in the record's `Gallery` group, are **Window Settings**, as ADR-0038 defines them: the **Thumbnail Stretch** of the **Docked Gallery** and of the **Expanded Gallery** (`DockedGalleryStretchMode`, `ExpandedGalleryStretchMode`), their item sizes (`DockedGalleryItemSize`, `ExpandedGalleryItemSize`), the **Gallery Display Mode** (`ExpandedGalleryDisplayMode`) and which of Masonry and Rows was used last (`RowsWasLastUsedDisplayMode`), the edge every Docked Gallery of the window sits at (`DockPosition`), and whether a new tab of the window starts docked (`StartsDocked`, stored as the record's `IsGalleryDocked`). They live in `WindowGallerySettings` (`PicView.Core/ViewModels`), which each window's `WindowSettings` owns as `WindowSettings.Gallery`: `WindowSettings.FromRecord` seeds it through `WindowGallerySettings.FromRecord`, `WindowSettings.CopyFor` gives a window opened from another an unstarted copy (`WindowGallerySettings.Copy`), `WindowSettings.Store` writes the eight back through `StoreTo` only when the window closes, and `WindowSettings.Dispose` disposes it. Seven are bindables; `RowsWasLastUsedDisplayMode` is a plain value that only a change of display mode updates. Changing one in a window changes nothing in another, and nothing writes the record or saves on a change. The four commands the gallery's menus and the Settings window run, `SetExpandedDisplayModeCommand`, `ToggleExpandedDisplayModeCommand`, `SetDockedStretchModeCommand` and `SetExpandedStretchModeCommand`, are on it too and start in `WindowGallerySettings.Start()`. `MainWindowLifecycle.SetUpShownWindow` calls it beside `WindowModeSwitcher.Start` for every window it shows, at App Start, from `OpenNewAsync` and from `OpenForMovedTabAsync`, so a copied window starts its own. It is idempotent, safe from any thread, and does nothing once the settings are disposed; it is not in the constructor because a window's settings only read their seed when made (ADR-0005), as `GlobalSettingsViewModel` does (ADR-0034). The item sizes are held to the `GalleryDefaults` limits: at the seed, in what `Look` publishes, and, once started, on each change, which sets a size outside them back. `WindowGallerySettings.Look` publishes the six values that shape a gallery as one `GalleryLook` (`PicView.Core/Gallery`): the two item sizes, the two stretches, the display mode and the dock edge. `GalleryLook.ThumbnailHeight` is the height the gallery's thumbnails are asked for at, through `Thumbnails.GalleryHeight(dockedItemSize, expandedItemSize)`, which no longer reads the record; the gallery's coordinator and the tab's Stand-in take it from the tab's look. A tab follows its window's look through `TabViewModel.FollowWindowSetting(static w => w.Gallery.Look, WindowGallerySettings.LookWithoutWindow(...))`, which the tab's constructor hands to `GalleryViewModel.FollowLook`; the gallery keeps it as `GalleryViewModel.Look` and derives its Gallery Appearance and its dock flags from it, with the app-wide item spacing. `GalleryViewModel.Initialize` still subscribes the gallery's own commands, once: a second call does nothing, and a move never calls it. A tab moved to a window with another look takes it in one change, so it publishes at most one new Gallery Appearance; one moved to a window that agrees does nothing, as the gallery's look property drops an equal value. When a tab enters another window, its `GalleryCoordinator` checks on its queue whether the new look needs taller thumbnails than the height its items were loaded at; if it does, the coordinator drops the items and the tab's kept thumbnails once and loads them again at the new height when the gallery is shown, and otherwise keeps them.

The **Gallery Mode** stays per tab (ADR-0011), and `GalleryModeSwitcher` stays its one writer. What it remembers goes to the window the tab is in at that moment, which the tab gives it through `GalleryModeSwitcher.RemembersIn` and which it asks at each write. `ToggleDocked`, `Contract` from Docked, `Close` and `DockAt` are the user's choices by hand, and each sets the window's `StartsDocked` to whether the tab is now docked, while the gallery is not hidden. `DockAt` also sets the window's `DockPosition`, even while hidden, which moves every Docked Gallery of that window and none of another. `ToggleExpanded`, `Contract` from Expanded, `SetHidden`, `SetOverLimit` and `Start` remember nothing, so crop, a Single Image and the **Gallery File Limit** leave the window's choice as it was. `GalleryModeSwitcher.Start()` starts a tab docked or closed as its window's `StartsDocked` says; `TabNavigationInitializer.Initialize` and `QuickLoad.LoadFileAsync` call it. A switcher whose tab has no window, or whose window has closed, remembers nothing and throws nothing: each write checks `WindowGallerySettings.IsDisposed` and catches `ObjectDisposedException` for a close between the check and the set, the semantics of `ToggleHelper.ChangeWindowSetting`, which lives in Avalonia where Core cannot call it. Its `Start()` then reads the record through `WindowGallerySettings.StartsDockedWithoutWindow`. A gallery whose tab has no window shows the record's look through `WindowGallerySettings.LookWithoutWindow`; one whose window has closed keeps the look it last had, as `FollowWindowSetting` goes silent. The dock edge is one field of the look, so it has no follower or poll of its own, and a tab moved to a window that differs in look and edge publishes one Gallery Appearance. `DockAt` on a Closed, shown gallery would publish two, a Closed one at the new edge and then the Docked one, as the Closed appearance carries the edge. The switcher sets `IsMovingTheEdgeToDock` while it writes the edge, the tab's gallery publishes no appearance while it is set (`GalleryViewModel.PublishAppearance`), and the dock that follows publishes the one appearance at the new edge. That relies on the window's change reaching the tab's gallery inside the write, which the window-to-tab fan-out does today because every step of it is synchronous.

The Settings window belongs to no Main Window (ADR-0038), so its gallery page shows and edits the Active Main Window's values. `GallerySettingsView` follows `MainWindows.ActiveWindow` through `WindowSettings.Follow(s => s.Gallery.Look)` inside a `Switch`, so it follows a change of Active Main Window and goes silent for none or a closed one. It shows the look in its three combo boxes under a guard, so showing a value issues no command, and runs the Active Main Window's commands when the user picks one. Its two item size sliders bind two-way to `MainWindows.ActiveWindow.Value.WindowSettings.Gallery.DockedGalleryItemSize` and `ExpandedGalleryItemSize`, which no Action writes (ADR-0020). Its dock buttons run the active tab's `DockAtCommand`, as before. `WindowSettings.Follow` is the guarded subscribe that was `TabViewModel`'s private `SettingOf`, now public so the page uses the same mechanism as a tab. The gallery's item size popup, `GalleryItemSizeSlider`, is given its window's `WindowGallerySettings` and writes that window's size. Five values of the `Gallery` group stay **App Settings**: the item spacing and line spacing (`ItemSpacing`, `LineSpacing`), which the owner chose to keep app-wide, and the motion photo badge (`ShowMotionPhotoBadges`), the gallery's mouse wheel behavior (`GalleryMouseWheelBehavior`) and the Gallery File Limit (`HideGalleryAtFileCount`), which are preferences or behavior, not how one window's gallery looks. The first three stay on `GallerySharedSettingsViewModel` with their write-through and coalesced save, `GalleryMouseWheelBehavior` stays on `GlobalSettingsViewModel`, and the coordinator reads the file limit from the record. `WindowSettingRecordReadsTests` has a fourth scan, which fails on any read or write of the eight record properties through the record's `Gallery` group outside `WindowSettings`, `WindowGallerySettings`, `AppSettings` and `SettingsManager`.

Before this, one `GallerySharedSettingsViewModel` held the two stretches, the two item sizes and the display mode, with write-throughs and a coalesced save, and every window's galleries and the Settings page bound that one object, and the item size popup wrote to it, so a change in one Main Window changed the galleries of all of them and was saved at once. The dock edge was published by nothing: `GalleryViewModel.Initialize` polled `Settings.Gallery.DockPosition` with `Observable.EveryValueChanged`, and `GalleryModeSwitcher` wrote `DockPosition` and `IsGalleryDocked` to the record and saved through a delegate it was made with, so a dock by hand in any tab of any window decided how the next tab of every window started. `Thumbnails.GalleryHeight` read both item sizes from the record.

## Considered Options

- **The eight directly on `WindowSettings`.** One owner less, but `WindowSettings` would also carry the gallery's four commands, its twelve derived flags and their start. In their own object they are seeded, copied, stored, started and disposed as one group, and the gallery's XAML binds `WindowSettings.Gallery.X` where it bound `GallerySettings.X`, a change of prefix only.
- **Gallery Mode per window.** ADR-0011 rejected docked state per Main Window: the tabs of one window would disturb each other, and each tab already has its own gallery view. That holds. Only what a new tab starts in and where a Docked Gallery sits are the window's; whether a tab's gallery is closed, docked or expanded is still the tab's.
- **Ask again for taller thumbnails on every item size change.** The sliders write on every step of a drag, so each step would drop and reload the items. It would need a delay or a re-ask when the slider closes, and an item size raised in a window never asked again before this either.

## Consequences

- This completes the Window Settings design of ADR-0038, ADR-0039, ADR-0040 and this ADR: every Setting in the design's table of Window Settings is now one, and every other Setting is an App Setting.
- A crash loses the session's changes to these eight too, as ADR-0038 says of every Window Setting: nothing saves them on a change, so each window's values reach the record only when it closes, and at App Shutdown with several windows open only the most recently active one's are stored.
- The guard scan is name-based. It sees a record property only behind a receiver named as the record is in the tree (`Settings`, `settings`, `record`, `existingSettings`, `appSettings`, `AppSettings`, `loaded`, `config`, `appConfig`). It cannot see the group held in a local first (`var g = Settings.Gallery;`, then `g.DockPosition`), a receiver with another name, the group and its property split across lines, or a property pattern. Those are found in review. A new allowed file needs a reason in the test, as for the other scans.
- A taller thumbnail is asked for again only when a tab enters a window that needs one. Raising an item size in the same window keeps the loaded items at the height they were made for, as before this. Items the gallery makes after that, for a file inserted, replaced or relocated, are made at the live height, so one gallery can hold items of two heights until it loads again; that too was so before this.
- With no Active Main Window, the Settings page's moved controls show stale values: the combo boxes keep those of the last window they showed, and the sliders show no window's size. Nothing they do changes anything, and nothing throws.
- The item-size limits are held by the window's settings, which the shared object never did; before this only `SettingsManager.EnsureSettings` at load and the sliders' own limits held them. A size set on the bindable before `Start()` is not set back, though `Look` still publishes the accepted size; nothing sets one that early.
- `ToggleDocked`, `Contract`, `Close` and `DockAt` still return `ValueTask`, which is always completed now that the switcher saves nothing. Making them synchronous would change the input path's `OpenCloseDockedGallery` and about 35 awaits in the tests for no change in behavior.
- The switcher and `GalleryViewModel` take no save delegate. `CoreViewModel`'s `saveGallerySettings` stays, for `GallerySharedSettingsViewModel`.
- `IsMovingTheEdgeToDock` is a plain flag. A look change from another thread that landed while it is set would publish no appearance, and the dock that follows at once publishes the latest look; both normally run on the UI thread. The coordinator's queue and the Stand-in read the six-field `GalleryLook` on the thread pool, so a torn read could at worst give one item a height that mixes two looks.
- Other Closed tabs of the window each publish one Closed appearance at a new edge, as the Closed appearance carries it; the window's Docked tabs move at once.
- The gallery menus' check marks bind the window's derived flags (`IsDockedStretchUniform` and the rest) `Mode=OneWay`.
- `GallerySharedSettingsViewModel.Start()` starts only the write-throughs of the three staying settings; each window's gallery commands start in its own `WindowGallerySettings.Start()`.
- The settings file keeps its shape and its version; no migration.
- macOS and Linux were built, not run.
- Supersedes ADR-0011's "`Settings.Gallery.IsGalleryDocked` only remembers whether a new tab starts docked: the last mode the user chose by hand in any tab", its "written and saved", its app-wide dock position and its app-wide Thumbnail Stretch, item size and Gallery Display Mode; ADR-0020's gallery shared settings line as it applies to the stretches, the item sizes and the display mode; ADR-0031's record read in `Thumbnails.GalleryHeight`; ADR-0034's account of what `GallerySharedSettingsViewModel.Start()` starts; ADR-0038's and ADR-0040's "still App Settings" for the gallery's look. Updates ADR-0010: a tab that enters a window whose look needs taller thumbnails loads its Gallery Items again, though its file list did not change.
