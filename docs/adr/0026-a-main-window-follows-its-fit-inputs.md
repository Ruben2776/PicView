# A Main Window Follows Its Fit Inputs

Each **Main Window** fits its active tab's image, and is to place itself, by following the inputs of the **Fit** itself: nothing outside its fitter, `WindowFitter` in `PicView.Avalonia/WindowBehavior`, asks it to fit, and once the placement branch lands nothing asks it to center. The fitter subscribes once, in `Follow()`, attached in `MainWindowLifecycle.Create` before the Window Mode is applied, to everything the Fit reads: the active tab and its Orientation, the tab strip, the Interface Visibility (ADR-0021), the Window Mode, the zoom to fit, scrolling and side by side settings and the Screen Margin setting (ADR-0020), the **Shown** change of every tab of the window for a new image (ADR-0014), the active tab's **Gallery Appearance** (ADR-0011) and whether its Expanded Gallery returns to Docked, its **Tab View** (ADR-0022), the window's screen through its scaling and position changes, and, for a window that keeps its size, the laid-out bounds of its content. Changes that come together give one Fit: a new image at Send priority, so the image never renders a frame at the old size; everything else at Normal, before layout. The fitter publishes each Fit it applies with its cause, a new image or another input, as `Fits`; `NavigationSubscriptions` follows that once per window to reset the viewer's transform (when the zoom resets on image change) and lay its motion photo overlay after a new image, which keeps the order ADR-0016 wants, Fit then reset, by construction. A new image that could not be fitted, as with a minimized window, no sizes or a plan of size 0, or that a tab showed while another tab was active, is owed by that tab: the next Fit that applies for it while it is active publishes as a new image, whatever input asked for it, so the control's scale still meets the tab's Zoom Factor. The window's **Placement** is to be part of the same rule, and that part lands with the placement branch `claude/fit-placement` (part B of the design): at this decision's head `QuickLoad`, `SourceOpener`, `WindowFunctions`, the slideshow and `MacWindowModeAdapter` still center the window, and `MainWindow.HandleWindowResize` still places it after a resize. Under that rule an AutoFit window is placed after each change of its size, a Fit included, centered in its Work Area with Stay Centered, else on its own center; turning AutoFit on places it the same way, and turning it off moves it nowhere; returning to Normal from fullscreen or maximized centers an AutoFit window on the screen it is on and gives a window that keeps its size its previous size and position back; a fullscreen or maximized window, and otherwise one that keeps its size, is not moved. Where a window opens is to be its opener's, with the same branch: at App Start an AutoFit window opens centered on its screen, through its startup location, a window that keeps its size at its last position, and a fullscreen or maximized window in that state on its last screen; a window opened while PicView runs opens on top of its opener, and one for a dragged-out tab at the cursor. App Start keeps its entries, `FitBeforeShowing` and `FitStartUpMenu` (ADR-0005, ADR-0022), the mode switcher keeps `FollowContent` and `KeepSize` (ADR-0006), and once the placement branch lands the user's Center action is the one outside caller of `Center`.

Before this, the fitter followed eight inputs and 19 sites in 11 files judged "the fit is stale now" themselves: `GalleryAnimationControl` four times, once per rendered frame of the Expanded to Docked tween; `UpdateImage` three times, including the reaction to every new image, where it had to run before the viewer's zoom reset; `MainWindow` three times, for a scaling change, a monitor drag and a resize by hand; `CropService` twice, with the crop's aspect ratio read right after; and one each in `SourceOpener` (a second Fit per open, after the reaction's), `QuickLoad` (a third for a startup pair), `MainWindowLifecycle` (a fullscreen start, posted at ApplicationIdle because the fitter was attached after the mode was applied and skipped its starting value), `WindowInitializer` (the Screen Margin, looping over every open window), `WindowFunctions` (a title-bar drag to another screen), `RotationManager` (a zoom reset, though zoom never changes an AutoFit window's size) and `CropLayoutManager` (a repair when the Fit had not run before layout). The window was centered from 16 sites in 9 files through two entries, `Center` and `WindowFunctions.CenterWindowOnScreen`, in six dispatcher styles, and every open with AutoFit on re-centered the window whether or not Stay Centered was on, while Next kept its center. The gallery's size came from the settings through `GalleryHelper.GetGallerySize`, not from the tab's Gallery Appearance, so a size-slider change in Docked mode fitted nothing. None of the outside calls had a test.

## Considered Options

- **The reaction keeps its one explicit `Refit`** and only the other 18 go. The fitter's remark would stay true for one input, and the order "Fit, then the control's reset" would stay a fact of one caller's body rather than of the fitter. Chosen instead: the fitter follows Shown and publishes the Fit; the viewer follows the Fit.
- **Both the fitter and the viewer subscribe to Shown**, with the fitter subscribing first. The order would hold by subscription order, which nothing names and a refactor can swap.
- **`Fits` publishes the Fit alone**, and the viewer remembers a pending reset from the Shown change and consumes it on the next Fit. One rule over two subscriptions; the cause on the published value says it in one.
- **One Normal-priority coalescer for every input**, the new image included. A frame could render the new image at the old size, which the Send-priority reaction prevented; the fitter keeps one coalescing flag per priority.
- **The gallery keeps an internal entry on the fitter for "content is animating"**, or the per-frame fit is dropped so the image jumps once at the end of the tween. A window that keeps its size already fits again after layout; following its content's bounds is that input made explicit, and the gallery stops knowing the fitter exists.
- **The crop reads the Fit Scale right after a synchronous fit**, as it did. The crop's aspect ratio follows the tab's Fit Scale instead, so no caller needs a Fit to have run.
- **Opening a file keeps re-centering an AutoFit window** with Stay Centered off, as `SourceOpener` and `QuickLoad` did. Stay Centered is the setting that means "center me"; an open that centered without it was the one path that disagreed with Next, which kept the window's center.
- **An AutoFit window at App Start opens at its saved position unless Stay Centered is on**, as the start-up menu path did, while a file start centered it. The two start paths disagreed; an AutoFit window has no center of its own yet when it first shows, so it is centered once, and only a window that keeps its size opens where it was.
- **Centering once when AutoFit turns on**, as `WindowFunctions` did whether or not Stay Centered was on. It is a resize of a window that already has a center of its own, so the after-resize rule places it: its own center, or the Work Area's with Stay Centered. Returning to Normal is different: a fullscreen or maximized window has no Normal center to keep, so with AutoFit it is centered on its screen, as the macOS adapter and the slideshow did on their own, now on every platform.
- **The macOS fullscreen turn's re-center folds into the placement rule** as "in Fullscreen on macOS, re-center after a Fit". Placement does nothing in Fullscreen; that line is a workaround for a quirk nobody can reproduce here and stays marked as one, outside the rule, in the spirit of ADR-0006.

## Consequences

- A new input to the Fit is a subscription inside `Follow()`, never a call from where the input changed. `Follow()` replaced `FollowFitInputs()`. `Refit` is deleted, since nothing inside the fitter called it either. `PlaceAfterResize` stays public until the placement branch takes it. The fitter is `IDisposable` and the window disposes it; its posted Fits return once it is disposed, so a Fit pending while the window tears its tabs down throws nothing.
- The reaction in `UpdateImage.ReactToShown` is gone. The tab resets its Orientation in `Publish` under the condition it resets its Zoom Factor (ADR-0016), and always in `DisplaySingleImage`, so the Orientation input and the Shown input both reach the fitter before its Fit; `ImageViewer.ShowFlip` posts instead of invoking, since that reset now runs on the loading thread. `TabOrientation` so has two writer threads, the loading thread's reset in `Publish` and the UI thread's turns and flips, the same shape as ADR-0016's factor reset; `ShowFlip` reads the Orientation when its posted job runs, so the one written last is the one shown. `Fits` carries `Fitted(Fit, FitCause)` from `PicView.Core/Sizing/Fitted.cs`, with `FitCause` new image or input; the viewer's reset and overlay follow it with cause new image. `SetSingleImage` displays the Single Image and disposes the iterator; its fit, reset, overlay and centering go.
- The Reserved Space reads the active tab's Gallery Appearance through `GalleryDocking.ReservedBy` in `PicView.Core/Gallery`: its Extent as a width for a side dock and a height for a top or bottom dock, when its mode is Docked and it is shown. An Expanded Gallery that returns to Docked reserves the docked thickness it returns to, its Appearance's `DockedExtent`, so an AutoFit window stays still while the gallery expands from Docked and closes back to it, as it did before; one that returns to Closed reserves nothing. `TooltipHelper` reads the rule for a Docked Gallery only. `GalleryHelper.GetGallerySize`, `GalleryTransition`'s `RefitsAtEnd`, `RefitsEveryFrame` and the `dock` and `autoFit` parameters of `For`, and `GalleryAnimationControl`'s `TweenWhileRefitting` go: the per-frame refit computed the same Fit every frame, since its Reserved Space and its content's size did not change during the tween, and the Gallery Appearance input gives that Fit once, as the tween starts. A Fixed Window gets two Input fits per input, one before the frame and one after layout.
- The crop's aspect ratio follows the tab's Fit Scale: `CropViewModel` takes the tab's `TabZoom` and reads `FitScale`, and `CropLayoutManager` keeps only a plain return for a NaN. `CropService` sets and clears `Crop` and nothing else about the window; the Tab View input fits when the gallery hides and returns; the Fit lands at Normal priority, before the crop control lays out at Render, so `CropLayoutManager` needs no repair fit.
- The fitter is attached in `Create`, after `SetViewFactory` and before `Show()` and `WindowModeSwitcher.Start`, so the starting mode is an input like any later one. A fullscreen Fit reads the screen, so a fullscreen start needs no later input; a maximized start, whose native size arrives after its first Fit, is what the content's bounds input covers, since a maximized window keeps its size. The ApplicationIdle fit at a fullscreen start and `RotationManager.ResetZoom`'s refit go; a fullscreen start is in the hand check. The constructor takes two test seams beside `readContentSize` and `readScreen`: `contentBounds`, by default the main view's bounds, and `screenChanges`, by default scaling merged with position changes; the screen input fits only when `IsOnAnotherScreen()`.
- The Screen Margin, `WindowMargin`, joins the published settings of ADR-0020 on `GlobalSettingsViewModel`, through a generic `Follow<T>`, with ADR-0020's write-through and coalesced save; `SettingsViewModel.WindowMargin` forwards to it, the Settings window binds to that and loops over no windows.
- The placement part lands with the placement branch `claude/fit-placement` (part B), not at this decision's head. With it, `WindowFunctions.CenterWindowOnScreen` stays for the user's Center action and the marked macOS workaround; App Start centers an AutoFit window through its startup location, as before; every other centering goes. The fitter's placement rule is to be the after-resize placement, moved into it from `MainWindow.HandleWindowResize`, plus one "once" case it follows through the Window Mode: the return to Normal with AutoFit. A window opened while PicView runs is to be placed by its opener, on top of it or at the cursor.
- The debt of a new image's Fit is per tab, since each tab keeps its own viewer (ADR-0022). The fitter follows the Shown change of every tab in the window's tabs collection, subscribing as a tab is added and letting go once it has left; a reorder takes a tab out and puts it back, so a tab's debt is dropped only once it is no longer among the window's tabs. A tab that shows a new image while another is active, as when the folder watcher replaces its shown file or the user switches tabs before its image is decoded, owes its Fit, and the first Fit that applies for it once it is active, the switch's Fit included, publishes as a new image, so its viewer resets and its motion photo overlay follows its image then, as the per-tab reaction did before. A tab that leaves the window, closed or moved, drops its debt.
- The fitter has a test per input and per placement case, through the headless `TestMainWindow`, and `Fits` is the assertion surface for the viewer's order; the 14 `FollowFitInputs` tests survive under `Follow`. ADR-0005, ADR-0014, ADR-0016, ADR-0021 and ADR-0022 hold; ADR-0022's "`Refit` keeps fitting a shown menu when the fit inputs change" reads "the fitter keeps fitting".
